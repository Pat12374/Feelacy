AI and Recommendation Transparency Notice
1. Scope
WineBloom may use machine learning and generative AI for search, product matching, ranking, recommendations, review summaries, catalog enrichment, fraud prevention, support, voice interfaces, and merchant tools. This notice describes general operation and choices; it does not disclose security-sensitive details or third-party trade secrets. A restriction under this section will not be applied more broadly or for longer than reasonably necessary for its lawful purpose.
2. Main ranking parameters
Product ranking may give substantial weight to query relevance, availability and lawful deliverability, user preferences, prior interactions, price and value signals, merchant service quality, review quality, popularity, freshness, diversity, and commercial context. The relative importance changes by feature. Sponsored results are labeled and payment does not convert an advertisement into an organic recommendation. Where consent is the legal basis, it must be specific, informed, freely given, recorded, and as easy to withdraw as to provide.
3. Personalization choices
When personalization is active, WineBloom may use saved preferences, searches, clicks, purchases, follows, location, language, price range, ratings, and consented device signals. Users can adjust preferences, clear history where offered, opt out of qualifying profiling, or choose a non-personalized feed where required. Non-personalized ranking may rely on current query, region, availability, and general popularity. Required disclosures must be clear, timely, accessible, and presented before the user makes the decision they are intended to inform.
4. Generative features
AI answers and summaries predict useful text from available inputs and may be incomplete or wrong. They are not professional, medical, legal, investment, authentication, or safety advice. Users should verify vintage, allergens, alcohol content, price, availability, merchant, delivery restrictions, and other material facts on the product and checkout pages. WineBloom and the relevant merchant must preserve evidence reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance and resolve a good-faith complaint.
5. User inputs and outputs
Users should not submit confidential, privileged, infringing, unlawful, or unnecessary personal information. Inputs and outputs may be processed to deliver, secure, evaluate, and improve features according to the Privacy Policy and settings. WineBloom does not promise exclusivity or ownership in machine-generated output; similar outputs may be produced for others. WineBloom and the relevant merchant must preserve evidence reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance and resolve a good-faith complaint.
6. Human oversight
WineBloom uses testing, monitoring, escalation, access controls, and human review proportionate to risk. High-impact decisions involving account access, fraud, merchant enforcement, or legal rights should have contest and human-review paths when applicable. AI assists staff but does not eliminate WineBloom’s responsibility for its own legal obligations. Required disclosures must be clear, timely, accessible, and presented before the user makes the decision they are intended to inform.
7. Fairness safety and alcohol
Models should be evaluated for quality, bias, manipulation, unsafe alcohol promotion, youth exposure, and disparate errors. WineBloom does not intentionally personalize alcohol marketing to known minors or use sensitive health or vulnerability traits to encourage harmful consumption. Safety filters may limit queries or results. Required disclosures must be clear, timely, accessible, and presented before the user makes the decision they are intended to inform.
8. Reviews and catalog data
Machine-generated summaries may synthesize multiple reviews and will be labeled. They should not invent consensus, conceal material negative themes, or be presented as a user quotation. Catalog enrichment may infer categories or tasting attributes, which should be distinguished from producer or merchant claims and corrected when reliable evidence shows error. WineBloom and the relevant merchant must preserve evidence reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance and resolve a good-faith complaint.
9. Merchant and sponsored influence
Merchant price, inventory, shipping reach, performance, and promotion can affect visibility. Paid placements, affiliate relationships, and house products must be distinguishable. Merchants may not manipulate inputs, reviews, or metadata to game models. WineBloom may demote deceptive optimization and explain material business-user ranking terms where law requires. Where consent is the legal basis, it must be specific, informed, freely given, recorded, and as easy to withdraw as to provide.
10. Privacy and automated decisions
Personal data used by AI is governed by the Privacy Policy. EEA and UK individuals may have rights concerning solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, including information, human intervention, and contest. Covered U.S. residents may opt out of certain profiling. Requests go to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. 11. Feedback and changes
Users can report harmful or inaccurate output at [AI FEEDBACK URL]. WineBloom may update models, vendors, parameters, and interfaces; material changes to user rights or data use receive appropriate notice. Version and evaluation records should support audits and regulator inquiries.
